# Phase 1 Due Diligence

## Scope
This memo covers only Phase 1 of the audit:

1. Consistency between repository documentation, specs, code, committed artifacts, and manuscript.
2. Basic traceability of headline counts and roster definitions.

No external sources were used. All findings below are based only on files inside this repository.

## Executive Summary
The repository is not internally consistent enough to be treated as publication-ready.

The dominant issue is specification drift. The project appears to have evolved materially, but the `README`, `specs`, scripts, committed data artifacts, and paper were not fully reconciled after those changes. The result is not one isolated mismatch but a pattern of overlapping, partially outdated descriptions.

The highest-risk finding is that the point-in-time universe described in `specs/08_universe.md` is no longer the same universe reflected by the committed data and the manuscript. A second high-risk finding is that the headline strategy roster differs across the specs, the rerun script, the result CSV, and the paper-facing README.

## Findings

### Finding 1. Test-count documentation is inconsistent
Severity: Medium

Evidence:
- `README.md` says `89 passed` and `89 tests` at lines 44, 69, and 112.
- `paper/ucema_journal/README.md` says `79/79 must pass` at line 40.
- Structural test count from the repository test files is 89 test functions:
  - `tests/test_inference.py`: 13
  - `tests/test_embeddings.py`: 10
  - `tests/test_hrp_variants.py`: 30
  - `tests/test_backtest.py`: 11
  - `tests/test_comparators.py`: 9
  - `tests/test_denoising.py`: 16

Assessment:
- The repository state supports the `89` count, not the `79/79` paper README claim.

Required remediation:
- Update `paper/ucema_journal/README.md` to match the repository's current test inventory.

### Finding 2. The strategy roster is not defined consistently across the repo
Severity: High

Evidence:
- `README.md` describes `22 portfolio strategies` at lines 4-8.
- `specs/README.md` still describes Backtest v2 as `20 strategies` at line 12.
- `specs/09_inference.md` still says `20 strategies × 2 scenarios` at lines 13-14.
- `specs/03_backtest_v2.md` defines a `24 strategies` headline comparison at lines 53-86.
- `paper/ucema_journal/README.md` says `19-strategy Scenario B table` at line 24.
- `scripts/rerun_all.py` says `19 strategies` in its docstring at line 8 and `strategy roster (19 + HODL)` at line 58.
- The actual `FACTORIES` roster in `scripts/rerun_all.py` contains 21 strategies at lines 59-86.
- The committed `data/backtest_v2_rebalance_results.csv` contains 23 rows: 21 `FACTORIES` names plus `HODL_BTC` plus `NCO_RT`.

Observed headline CSV roster:
- `HRP`
- `HRP_Detoned`
- `HRP_PartialCorr`
- `HRP_Dynamic_94`
- `HRP_TailDep`
- `HRP_TailDepShrunk`
- `HRP_ShrunkCov`
- `HRP_VolStd`
- `IVP`
- `MVP`
- `ERC`
- `MaxDiv`
- `CS_MOM_eq21`
- `RM_MOM`
- `MOM_HRP`
- `HERC`
- `NCO`
- `HRP_PathSig`
- `HRP_NodeEmbed`
- `HRP_Contrastive`
- `HRP_TS2Vec`
- `HODL_BTC`
- `NCO_RT`

Assessment:
- There is no single canonical roster definition currently enforced across the repository.
- The phrase `22 strategies` is only true if `HODL_BTC` is treated as a benchmark and not counted as a strategy, while `NCO_RT` is included. The paper itself uses exactly that mixed framing.

Required remediation:
- Create one canonical roster table with three explicit fields:
  - `name`
  - `type` (`strategy` or `benchmark`)
  - `included_in_headline` (`yes/no`)
- Reconcile `README`, `specs`, `scripts/rerun_all.py`, `paper/ucema_journal/README.md`, and `main.tex` against that table.

### Finding 3. The headline results artifact is not generated by `rerun_all.py` alone
Severity: High

Evidence:
- `scripts/rerun_all.py` does not include `NCO_RT` in `FACTORIES`.
- `scripts/add_nco_rt.py` explicitly states that `NCO_RT` is added after the main rerun and then spliced into existing metric files and inference outputs at lines 1-8.
- `scripts/add_nco_rt.py` appends `NCO_RT` into:
  - `data/backtest_v2_rebalance_results.csv` at lines 88-91
  - `data/inference_sharpe_diff.csv` and `data/inference_spa.csv` at lines 93-97
  - `data/backtest_v2_post_covid_results.csv` at lines 110-113
  - the corresponding post-COVID inference files at lines 115-126

Assessment:
- The repository's headline paper artifacts are not produced by one single authoritative rerun script.
- The current published state depends on a two-step process: `rerun_all.py` then `add_nco_rt.py`.
- This is not necessarily wrong, but it is not documented clearly enough in the paper-facing materials.

Required remediation:
- Either merge `NCO_RT` into the main rerun pipeline or make the two-step process explicit in all reproduction instructions.

### Finding 4. `specs/08_universe.md` is materially stale relative to the implemented and published universe
Severity: High

Evidence:
- `specs/08_universe.md` describes a `91 unique symbols` dataset and `87 ever-included` symbols at lines 35-42.
- The committed `data/pit_universe.csv` yields:
  - `146` ever-included symbols
  - `97` symbols dropped during the window
  - `100` snapshots
  - last snapshot date `2026-05-31`
  - max included universe size `51`
- `paper/ucema_journal/main.tex` states `146 ever-included symbols` at lines 45 and 1150.
- `specs/03_backtest_v2.md` still repeats the older `91 unique / 87 ever-included / 38 dropped` counts at lines 38-40.

Assessment:
- The universe spec and backtest spec no longer describe the committed PIT universe used by the paper.
- This is the most consequential documentation drift in the repository.

Required remediation:
- Rewrite `specs/08_universe.md` and the universe-dependent sections of `specs/03_backtest_v2.md` to reflect the actual committed PIT dataset.

### Finding 5. Universe-selection rules are internally contradictory even within `specs/08_universe.md`
Severity: High

Evidence:
- `specs/08_universe.md` requires top `50` by rolling quote volume at lines 78-84.
- The same spec still says entry/exit buffering is based on entering and leaving `top 30` at lines 91-95.
- The same spec still asks whether to choose `Top-30 vs Top-20 vs Top-40` and recommends `Top-30` in open question OQ1 at lines 200-202.
- The same spec still sets acceptance criterion `AC2` to median universe size `30 ± 3` at lines 167-168.
- The implementation default in `src/universe.py` remains `top_n: int = 30` at line 253.

Assessment:
- This is not a small editorial mismatch. The spec simultaneously describes a top-50 and top-30 world.
- The implementation signature also still points to the old default.

Required remediation:
- Make top-N a single, frozen, canonical choice for the headline pipeline.
- Update the implementation default or require explicit passing everywhere.

### Finding 6. `specs/08_universe.md` still mixes deprecated CoinGecko design with the current Binance quote-volume method
Severity: High

Evidence:
- The methodology note says the spec moved away from CoinGecko to Binance quote volume at lines 24-33.
- But `R4` still lists `Historical market-cap rankings: CoinGecko historical data` at lines 97-100.
- `R6` still says the PIT artifact columns include `market_cap` at lines 114-118.
- The interface section still defines `fetch_historical_market_caps(...)` and a `market_caps` input to `build_pit_universe(...)` at lines 124-142.
- `src/universe.py` no longer exposes that interface. The actual implemented workflow is based on price/volume snapshots and listing metadata.

Assessment:
- `specs/08_universe.md` cannot currently be treated as the governing contract for the implemented universe.

Required remediation:
- Split historical context from current contract.
- Remove or archive the deprecated CoinGecko interface from the active spec.

### Finding 7. The implementation comments in `src/universe.py` also show unresolved terminology drift
Severity: Medium

Evidence:
- `src/universe.py` docstring for `to_monthly_snapshots` still says `rolling_quote_vol_30d is the median` at line 224.
- The implementation comment immediately below says the code now uses the `MEAN` and that median was the previous behavior at lines 228-234.
- `build_pit_universe(...)` still uses argument name `min_median_volume_usd` at line 255 and refers to `30d-median-volume` in the step description at line 265, even though the ranking variable is now a 30-day rolling mean of quote volume.

Assessment:
- The implementation may be correct, but its own inline documentation has not been fully cleaned up.

Required remediation:
- Normalize code comments, argument names, and docstrings to the actual implemented metric.

### Finding 9. The manuscript has at least one directly visible numerical inconsistency
Severity: Medium

Evidence:
- `paper/ucema_journal/main.tex` reports baseline HRP total return as `+417%` in Table 1 at line 539.
- The same manuscript reports baseline HRP total return as `+418%` in the sweep baseline row at line 772.
- The committed CSV value in `data/backtest_v2_rebalance_results.csv` is `4.174935828602413`, which rounds to `+417%`.

Assessment:
- This is a small inconsistency, but it is exactly the kind of thing that should be eliminated before publication submission.

Required remediation:
- Reconcile all rounded table values directly against the committed CSVs.

### Finding 10. The manuscript and committed results disagree with older spec timing and sample framing
Severity: Medium

Evidence:
- `specs/08_universe.md` says monthly snapshots span `Jan 2019 -> Dec 2023` at lines 75-76.
- The committed `pit_universe_summary.csv` starts at `2018-02-28` and has 100 snapshots.
- `main.tex` uses a `2020-02` to `2026-05` out-of-sample evaluation window in the headline table at lines 524-525.

Assessment:
- The specs reflect an earlier project phase, not the current published state.

Required remediation:
- Freeze one final timeline description across universe build, backtest sample, and paper.

## Secondary Observations

1. `specs/03_backtest_v2.md` contains a much larger design than the current committed headline pipeline, including strategies such as `HRP_Dynamic_97`, `HRP_Dynamic_99`, `NetworkRiskParity`, `TS_MOM`, `MOM_HRP_Detoned`, `Equal-Weight`, and `HODL_ETH` that are not present in the committed headline result CSV.
2. `specs/04_paper.md` still frames the paper around earlier datasets and earlier section designs, including `2018-12-31 -> 2024-01-01` at lines 91-95, which no longer reflects the manuscript's stated window.

## Phase 1 Conclusion
The repository currently contains enough internal inconsistency that a reviewer could reasonably question whether the manuscript, the specs, and the committed result artifacts all refer to the same final experiment set.

The strongest interpretation is not that the work is unreliable, but that the project evolved and the documentation contract was not re-frozen after major scope expansions.

Before deeper methodology audit, the repository should establish one canonical current-state definition for:

1. the PIT universe,
2. the headline strategy roster,
3. the rerun pipeline,
4. the sample window,
5. the test inventory,
6. the paper-to-artifact traceability map.
